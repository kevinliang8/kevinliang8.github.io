NACLO 2027 at McMaster University

McMaster University in Hamilton, Ontario is a host site for the North American Computational Linguistics Open competition (NACLO) in 2027. NACLO is a free contest for middle and high school students. You solve puzzles about languages you have probably never seen, such as working out how a writing system works or how a language forms its plurals from a handful of examples. You don’t need to know any linguistics or any foreign languages to take part. The top students in NACLO can go on to the International Linguistics Olympiad, which will be held in Bangkok, Thailand in July 2027.

How to register

  1. Create an account on the NACLO registration site, using an email address you check regularly.
  2. Register for the Open Round and choose McMaster University from the list of sites.

NACLO doesn’t assign sites, so you need to pick McMaster yourself. Please register in advance. Registering gives you a NACLO ID, which you will need on the day of the contest.

Who can take part

NACLO is open to middle and high school students who are US or Canadian citizens or who attend secondary school in the US or Canada, and who have never been enrolled full-time at a college or university. It is aimed mainly at students aged 13 to 18. Younger students can take part with a parent’s permission and should register with a parent’s or guardian’s email address.

On the day

  • Bring black pens or dark pencils and your NACLO ID. A quiet snack, water, and a basic wristwatch are fine.
  • Phones, calculators, computers, tablets, and smartwatches are not allowed during the contest. Turn them fully off.
  • Don’t bring books, notes, or your own paper. We provide paper.
  • After the contest, please don’t discuss the problems with anyone, including online.

The NACLO website has the full Student Handbook and more than 300 past problems for practice.
